The Club

Competitive badminton. Friendly atmosphere.

Monsoon Active is a Coventry badminton club with strong league teams and a social club atmosphere.

We're competitive on court, but social off it. Whether you're looking for competitive league badminton or simply a good night on court, you'll find a welcoming group of players here.

We play with feather shuttles and welcome visitors who want to come along and see what we're about.

We're primarily a competitive intermediate-to-advanced club, with players competing across Coventry League divisions.
